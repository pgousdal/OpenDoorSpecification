# Research Methodology

## Evidence levels

- **Verified:** directly stated in documentation, headers, source, or observed runtime behavior.
- **Inferred:** supported by examples or compatibility claims but not fully specified.
- **Unknown:** not established.

## Required provenance

Each archive record stores filename, size, SHA-256 hash, entry metadata, and acquisition status. Function and structure records will additionally cite internal file paths and line ranges or parser locations.

## Copyright

ODS stores original metadata, extracted declarations where legally appropriate, and concise factual summaries. Third-party binary archives and full documentation are not committed by default.
